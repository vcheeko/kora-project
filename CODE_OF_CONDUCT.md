# KORA Community Standards

KORA welcomes rigorous technical disagreement and expects respectful professional conduct.

## Expected behavior

When participating in this repository:

- critique ideas, architecture and evidence rather than attacking people;
- be specific about assumptions, risks and failure modes;
- distinguish facts, hypotheses and opinions;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest when they materially affect a proposal;
- respect privacy, confidentiality and intellectual-property boundaries;
- avoid publishing security-sensitive details in public issues;
- assume good faith, while still requiring evidence for technical claims;
- make space for contributors with different levels of technical experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

This repository does not accept:

- harassment, threats or personal attacks;
- discriminatory or degrading language;
- doxxing or publication of private personal information;
- deliberate disclosure of credentials, secrets or private security details;
- spam, scams or deceptive commercial solicitation;
- knowingly submitting proprietary material without permission;
- repeated disruption after maintainers have asked for a discussion to stop or move elsewhere.

## Technical disagreement

Strong disagreement is allowed and useful.

A good technical challenge explains:

1. what claim or design choice it disputes;
2. what evidence or reasoning supports the challenge;
3. what smaller or safer alternative is proposed;
4. how the alternative could be tested.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content and may restrict participation when behavior materially violates these standards or creates security, privacy, legal or collaboration risk.

For sensitive conduct or security concerns, do not post private details publicly. Use the private contact path described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or contact the founder through the profile links in the README and request a private channel.
